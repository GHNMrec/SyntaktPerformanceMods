SYNTAKT PERFORMANCE MODS
Source code (GitHub)

Build your own Syntakt OS with the Performance Mods

The JOG page (a CDJ / vinyl jog on [TEMPO]) and PERFORM (a smooth DJ filter over the whole mix with RESO and DRV knobs, drawn over its live spectrum). User guide

🔒 Everything happens in this browser tab. Your OS file is never uploaded, and this site hosts no firmware: you bring Elektron's official file, and the result is saved only to your own computer.

1Your official Syntakt OS

Drop your Syntakt OS .syx here, or click to choose it
The unmodified OS 1.41 or 1.42 from elektron.se (Support & Downloads, Syntakt). It's recognised by its SHA-256; anything else is refused.

2Choose what goes in

For a Syntakt already running Syntakt MAX (public 1.0, or a later MAX that has a manifest). Your MAX mods stay as they are; the Performance Mods go next to them. Nothing is added to the FX track's pages.

Drop your MAX manifest here (max-1.0-manifest.json)
MAX 1.0's Windows builder keeps it in its resources folder. It must match the OS file from step 1.

The build first rebuilds your MAX alone and checks it matches its own golden hash, so you know the files are right before anything is added. It takes about a minute.

3Build and save

Pick your OS file first.
    ✓ Built and verified.
    SHA-256

    Send it to the Syntakt with Elektron Transfer

    1. Back up your projects and samples first. Keep the official OS file at hand.
    2. Connect the Syntakt by USB and turn it on. Open Elektron Transfer.
    3. On the CONNECTION page, pick the Syntakt for MIDI IN and MIDI OUT.
    4. On the DROP page, drag in the .syx you just saved. Press [YES] on the Syntakt when it asks; a bar shows the progress, and the unit restarts by itself. Don't turn it off meanwhile.
    5. If something goes wrong: hold [FUNC] while powering on, [TRIG 4] = OS UPGRADE, and send the official OS with Transfer's legacy OS upgrade. That route needs a MIDI interface on the Syntakt's MIDI port (not USB).

    This file is yours: don't pass it on. Anyone else builds their own here from their own OS file.
